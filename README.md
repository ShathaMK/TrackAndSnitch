
<h1>Sneak or Seek 👀</h1>


is a game based on Saudi culture as back in the day they used to refer to foot print tracker to find the thief.



<br/>
<h3 align="left">Main objective:</h3>
Find the theif and the stolen item.
<h3>Gameplay Insturctions:</h3>

There is four roles in this game Tracker , Helper , Trickster and Thief.


Each role has a different goal.


As a Tracker your objective is to find who is the Thief and what is the stolen item.


As a Trickster your goal is to distract and mess with players.


As a Helper you know who is the Thief and you try to help him get away with it.


As a Thief your goal is not to be discovered.


The game requires a group of 4 - 10 players.
